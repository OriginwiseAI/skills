# Source-to-Video Templates

Free, copyable worksheets for teams turning an SOP, manual, technical guide, policy, or training source into a reviewable visual explanation.

These templates are deliberately source-led: they help a team agree on what the viewer needs to understand, which source is current, what must remain true, and where a human reviewer is needed. They are not a substitute for a controlled procedure, security runbook, legal policy, or domain expert.

## Included templates

1. [SOP-to-training-video review checklist](sop-training-video-review-checklist.md) — prepare the source, sequence, exceptions, reviewer, and update trigger.
2. [Source-to-video handoff sheet](source-to-video-handoff-sheet.md) — a one-page brief for a writer, designer, or production partner.
3. [Decision-point storyboard](decision-point-storyboard.md) — map a normal path, an evidence check, a stop condition, and the owner of the next action.

## How to use this pack

1. Copy the template that fits the job.
2. Complete it with the current source owner and a reviewer who can validate terminology, sequence, and boundaries.
3. Keep versioned values, access-controlled details, and deeper exception handling in the canonical source.
4. Use the approved brief to create or review the visual explanation.
5. Re-check the artifact when the source, policy, product, or process changes.

An [illustrative IT-triage walkthrough](https://kpainter.ai/examples/it-incident-triage-video) shows one way to make a role, decision, evidence, and handoff visible. It is a communication example, not a universal incident procedure.
